Welcome, Adventurers

DnD is a cooperative game where you work together to tell a story. The plan is to run a 3-part campaign, starting on a day we'll pick together (vote at the bottom of the page!). We'll use D&D Beyond, the official™ online campaign-management tool, to organize ourselves and roll our dice (unless people want to go the way of our forefathers, buy real dice, and physically roll them...).

The Story

1. The Clown of Kaer Morhen

Since your childhood at the School of the Wolf, you have suffered: brutal training, sleepless nights, agonizing alchemical potions, the Trial of the Grasses… but of all these things, nothing has hurt more than existing alongside Geralt of Rivia.

A drunk, a coward, and worst of all, a global celebrity. Each of his successes was just a fraudulent sham. I mean, think about it: his “girlfriend” Yennefer is only with him because he used the Djinn to convince her to date him. The “Butcher of Blaviken”: he fought two drunk ordinary people – with all the steroids, human growth hormone, and Witcher sense-enhancing potions (it's just dissolved amphetamine); even he couldn't lose that fight.

The time has come. Your band of witchers is finally striking out without the pretender Geralt and taking on the most dangerous contract the school has ever seen.
